01 — About

From networks to network defence

My foundation is enterprise networking — I build multi-layer campus networks with redundancy, dynamic routing and first-hop resilience, then harden them with ACLs, port security and secure management. That grounding is what pulled me into cybersecurity: you can't defend what you don't understand at the packet level.

I'm now a postgraduate cybersecurity student, working through the full offensive-security methodology in controlled labs — reconnaissance, exploitation, privilege escalation, persistence and post-exploitation — and mapping each step back to detection and prevention controls. Alongside that I automate with Ansible and Python, and I'm building toward cloud engineering and DevOps.

Networking
